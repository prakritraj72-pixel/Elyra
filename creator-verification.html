Creator Verification

Verification is part of ELYRA's trust and safety process for creator profiles.

01 · Activate

Complete the current one-time ₹20 creator activation payment and submit the transaction reference.

02 · Submit ID

Provide a supported government ID and a selfie through the secure verification flow.

03 · Manual review

An authorized reviewer checks the submitted information. Reviews usually take around 2 hours, but this is only an estimate.

04 · Go live

Your profile remains inactive until both the required payment and identity/age verification are approved.

Why is this required?

ELYRA uses creator verification to support user safety, scam/fraud prevention and platform trust. A Verified badge indicates that the required verification was approved; it is not a guarantee about a person's future behavior.

How we handle verification information

Your ID and selfie are used for identity and age verification and are not intended for unauthorized or unrelated use. Do not send passwords, OTPs or UPI PINs as part of verification.

If verification is rejected

If a submission is rejected, the verification flow may show a reason and allow you to correct the issue and resubmit where appropriate.
