Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use ELYRA?

ELYRA is strictly for people aged 18 and above.

Can I browse creators for free?

Yes. Browsing public creator profiles can be free. Customer booking requires an active ELYRA subscription.

What are the customer plans?

Current plans are ₹199 per month and ₹699 per year, subject to the terms shown at checkout.

How much does creator activation cost?

Creator profile activation currently uses a one-time ₹20 activation fee when required.

Does ELYRA charge a platform fee to creators?

ELYRA currently advertises 0% platform fee on creator bookings. This means creators currently keep 100% of their booking earnings, subject to the applicable payment and commercial terms.

What is ELYRA for?

ELYRA is for lawful social companionship and activities such as spending time together, exploring places or doing agreed activities.

Are sexual services allowed?

No. Sexual services, prostitution, exploitation, trafficking, coercion and other illegal activity are strictly prohibited.

How do I stay safe?

Prefer public meeting places, tell someone you trust where you are going, protect your private information and leave any situation that makes you uncomfortable. In an emergency in India, call 112.
