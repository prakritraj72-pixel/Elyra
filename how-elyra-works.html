How ELYRA Works

A simple flow for discovering people, building trust and arranging lawful social companionship.

For customers

01

Create your account

Join ELYRA and complete your basic profile information. ELYRA is strictly 18+.

02

Browse profiles

Explore available creator profiles, city/area, interests, availability and rates.

03

Get booking access

Maintain an active subscription and complete any required safety or verification checks.

04

Choose a booking

Select a suitable time and agree on a lawful activity and meeting details.

05

Meet safely

Prefer public places, tell someone you trust and use Safety Mode when available.

06

Report problems

Use report/block tools for concerns. In immediate danger in India, call 112.

For creators

01

Create your creator profile

Add your public profile details, photos, availability and lawful activity information.

02

Pay ₹20 activation

A one-time manual activation payment is currently required before activation can proceed.

03

Verify identity & age

Submit a supported government ID and selfie for manual review. This supports safety, scam/fraud prevention and platform trust.

04

Get approved

Review usually takes around 2 hours, but timing is an estimate. Your profile stays inactive until approval.

05

Go live

After payment and identity/age verification are approved, your creator profile can become active and display a Verified badge.

06

Keep boundaries clear

Accept only lawful, respectful bookings. Elyra currently advertises 0% platform fee on creator bookings.

Important: Verification is a trust and safety measure, not a guarantee that every future interaction is safe. Use your own judgment and the available safety tools.
