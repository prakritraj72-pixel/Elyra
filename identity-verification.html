Privacy & retention: Your government ID and selfie are collected only for Elyra identity and age verification. Verification files are stored in a private, access-controlled system and are automatically deleted from Elyra after 30 days from submission. The verification record is also automatically removed after the retention period. This process is designed to minimize how long sensitive documents are retained. This is manual identity verification, not automated government KYC, biometric face matching or liveness verification. Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment PINs to support.
