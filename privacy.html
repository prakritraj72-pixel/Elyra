Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ELYRA is an 18+ lawful social companionship and activity marketplace. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate accounts, profiles, bookings, payments and safety features.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use ELYRA, we may collect account information such as name, email address, phone number, age, city, area and login information. Creators may also provide profile photos, biography, availability, gender and hourly rate.

2. Booking information

When a booking is created, we may store booking date, time, duration, amount, notes and booking status. We use this information to operate the booking and provide relevant account features.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. ELYRA does not intend to store complete card, UPI PIN or banking credentials. Payment providers may process payment information under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Location

Where a location feature is offered, location access should be permission-based. Public creator discovery may use approximate city or area information. ELYRA should not publicly display a user's precise home address or exact real-time location.

5. How information is used

  • To create and manage accounts and creator profiles.
  • To provide profile discovery and booking services.
  • To process subscriptions and other payments.
  • To communicate important booking, account and safety notifications.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud and unlawful activity.
  • To improve reliability, security and user experience.

6. Information sharing

We may share necessary information with service providers that help operate authentication, hosting, databases, payments, communications and security. We do not intend to sell personal information to advertisers. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect users and the platform.

7. Public profiles

Creator profiles are designed to be discoverable by other users. Only information intended for public profile display should be shown publicly. Sensitive account credentials, private contact information and internal identifiers should not be exposed through public profile pages.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid posting passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive information in profiles or messages.

9. Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with your account. Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction purposes.

10. Children

ELYRA is strictly intended for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly provide the service to children. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact ELYRA so appropriate action can be taken.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact ELYRA at velora1906@gmail.com.

12. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ELYRA's services, technology or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
