Safety Guidelines

ELYRA is built for adults and lawful social companionship. Please use the platform responsibly.

Emergency: If you are in immediate danger in India, call 112. ELYRA's SOS feature can help initiate an emergency call, but it does not replace emergency services.

Meet safely

  • Prefer public or familiar locations for first meetings.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going.
  • Keep control of your own transport and belongings.
  • Trust your instincts and leave if you feel uncomfortable.

Protect your privacy

Do not share passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive information. Avoid posting your home address publicly.

Respect and consent

All interactions must be respectful and consensual. Harassment, threats, stalking, coercion and unwanted sexual conduct are not allowed.

Strictly prohibited

Sexual services, prostitution, trafficking, exploitation, meetings involving minors, illegal activities, fraud and other unlawful conduct are prohibited.

Report concerns

If you see a suspicious or abusive profile, preserve relevant information where safe to do so and report it through the platform. Serious or immediate threats should also be reported to the appropriate authorities.

Creator safety

Creators should avoid sharing their private residential address publicly and should use appropriate boundaries when communicating with customers. Do not accept requests that violate Elyra's rules or the law.
