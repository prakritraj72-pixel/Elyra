ELYRA

Safety Center

ELYRA is designed for adults and lawful social companionship. Safety is a shared responsibility between the platform and its users.

Emergency: If you are in immediate danger in India, call 112. ELYRA safety features do not replace police, ambulance or other emergency services.

Before you meet

Meet publicly

For a first meeting, prefer a public, familiar and reasonably busy place.

Tell someone

Share your plan, meeting place and expected timing with someone you trust.

Stay independent

Keep control of your transport, phone, money and important belongings.

Trust your instincts

You can end a meeting at any time. You do not owe anyone continued interaction.

Safety Mode

For eligible active bookings, Safety Mode is intended to provide a temporary safety session. Where enabled, it can support location updates and emergency alerts during the session. Location is not intended to become part of a public profile, and users should grant location permission only when they understand why it is being requested.

Protect your privacy

  • Never share your password, OTP, UPI PIN or banking credentials.
  • Do not publish your home address or unnecessary personal documents.
  • Be careful with private photos and personal contact details.
  • Do not send money because someone pressures or threatens you.

Respect, consent & boundaries

Every interaction must be respectful and consensual. Harassment, stalking, threats, coercion, unwanted sexual conduct or pressure to continue an interaction are not allowed.

Strictly prohibited

Sexual services, prostitution, trafficking, exploitation, meetings involving minors, fraud, impersonation and other unlawful activity are prohibited on ELYRA.

Report & block

If a profile or user seems suspicious, abusive or unsafe, use the platform's report/block features where available. Keep relevant booking or message information if it is safe to do so. Serious or immediate threats should also be reported to the appropriate authorities.

Creator safety

Creators should not publish their private residential address, should set clear boundaries and should decline requests that violate ELYRA rules or the law. Creator verification helps platform trust, but a verification badge is not a guarantee that every future interaction will be safe.
